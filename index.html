About this scene ＋

The Revit structure retains its approved version 2 position and geometry. The entrance passage and street frontage have been repaved. The former market outline has been removed.

Roads and the turning loop follow government drawing 11-SE-13B. The road continuation north of the CAD sheet is interpreted from the supplied 3D map. Street furniture and finishes interpret the reference photographs.

Three detailed Hong Kong double-decker trams follow the centre between the CAD rails. Vehicles leave and re-enter at the edge of the scene. Three moving cars follow the western streets; three cars are parked beside the garden. Use Pause traffic to stop all vehicles.
