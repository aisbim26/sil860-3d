About this scene ＋

The supplied Revit structure replaces the former Shau Kei Wan Market. The model has moved 2.80 m west and 0.55 m south to align with the CAD plan, retaining its scale, orientation and levels.

Rebuilt roads, kerbs and the tram turning loop follow government drawing 11-SE-13B. Rails follow the CAD curves. Paving, railings, poles and road markings interpret the supplied street photographs.

Plan view uses an orthographic camera. The gold line marks the former building footprint, not a legal lot boundary. Visual positioning remains subject to shared survey coordinates.

Five detailed trees remain near the site. The hillside uses 310 low-detail trees, screened against green map areas and CAD building footprints. The tram island tree has been removed. Tree positions and sizes are illustrative.

Map tiles: 11-SE-13B and 11-SE-8D. The eastern tile ends about 197 m from the centre. Source vegetation FBX files are excluded.
